Our thesis

The future of animation isn't just generation. It's creation.

Today's AI can generate images, video, voices, and other individual assets. We believe the bigger opportunity is connecting those capabilities into a creative system that understands the characters, stories, and worlds creators are building.

Context Creative work should build on what came before.
Consistency Characters and worlds should remain recognizable across stories.
Control AI should accelerate creators, not replace their creative decisions.
